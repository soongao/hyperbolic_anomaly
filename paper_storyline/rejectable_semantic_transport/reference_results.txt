Reference result notice
=======================

The numerical values in the current draft tables of main.tex and main_zh.tex are reference/planning values used to make the paper draft structurally complete. They are not measured experimental results. Future humans and agents should replace or verify them with real runs before submission.

Do not add this notice to the paper body. The manuscript should read as a complete paper draft; this sidecar file carries the status of the numbers.

Assumed protocol
----------------

- Task: CLIP-based zero-shot anomaly localization.
- Target datasets: MVTec AD and VisA.
- Reported table values: mean over MVTec AD and VisA.
- Target-domain data: no target anomaly samples.
- Backbone assumption: fixed CLIP/AnomalyCLIP-style patch features with compact normality anchors.
- Primary success metrics: P-AUPRO, P-AUROC, P-AP, I-AUROC, I-AP.

Success interpretation
----------------------

- Minimal success: HNA improves mean P-AUPRO by at least 1.0 over the closest prompt/adaptation baseline and shows targeted ablation drops.
- Solid success: HNA improves P-AUPRO and P-AUROC together, reduces normal FPR, and the hyperbolic normality acceptance plus UOT rows are both necessary.
- Strong success: HNA improves over recent CLIP ZSAD baselines on both MVTec AD and VisA, while transport visualizations show unaccepted mass concentrated on defects and low activation on difficult normal structures.
